SUMMARY
The Allotment Optimizer is a role within Revenue Optimization teams that carries the operational ownership of allotment processes. While the Capacity Analyst owns analysis and reporting, the Allotment Optimizer opens and closes the request period, evaluates incoming requests from ACRM, creates the approved allotments, and manages group/exception scenarios. COMIS CAP006 and CAP012 are used when needed.
1
ACRM operational ownership — Allotment processes are primarily managed through ACRM; COMIS is used when required.
2
Report ownership separation — Allotment utilization performance reports are viewed in ACRM but report ownership belongs to the Capacity Analyst team.
3
Exception and group management — Scenarios involving inclusion/exclusion of specific station customers and group customer/station allotments are managed by this team.

Goals, Motivation & Success Criteria

Primary Goals · Fears · Success / Failure Conditions
PRIMARY GOAL
🎯 Evaluate allotment requests on time and create the correct allotments — ensuring operational continuity during technical issues and exception scenarios
✅ Success Conditions
  • Allotment request period opened and closed on time
  • Evaluated allotments correctly created in ACRM
  • Group allotment and exception scenarios processed without errors
  • Operational support provided during technical issues
  • Allotment utilization rate at target level
❌ Failure Conditions
  • Exception scenario not fully supported in ACRM → complexity switching to COMIS
  • Group allotment conflict → incorrect capacity distribution
  • Request period opening late → delays and complaints in requests
  • ACRM-COMIS dual management difficulty → risk of configuration errors
🟢 Starts When
When a sales or station allotment request arrives / When the request period initiation time comes
🟡 Continues While
Request evaluation, allotment creation, group/exception management, technical support
🔴 Ends When
Allotment active, request period closed, performance tracking handed over to Capacity Analyst
SUMMARY
The Allotment Optimizer's primary motivation is ensuring the continuity and accuracy of allotment requests. The capacity to manage exception and group allotment scenarios is critical — these scenarios deviate from standard flow and push system limits. The key indicator of success is that allotments are created on time and without errors.

⚠️ EXCEPTION SCENARIO — Critical Detail

The scenario where customers of a specific station can use the allotment (or where some customers are excluded) is managed by this team. These configurations that deviate from standard flow may not be fully supported in ACRM; in this case COMIS CAP006 / CAP012 is used.

WORKFLOW SUMMARY
The Allotment Optimizer runs the entire operational flow from period management to allotment creation. The start and end of the process rests with this role. Standard allotments are created in ACRM; for group and exception scenarios, COMIS CAP006/CAP012 is used when needed. The first operational support during technical issues also comes from this team. Report ownership and ACRM-COMIS synchronization coordination remains with the Capacity Analyst.

Pain Points & Risk

Operational Challenges · System Limitations · Risk Areas
P1
Exception Scenario Limitations in ACRM

Exception scenarios involving inclusion/exclusion of specific customers are not fully supported in ACRM. In this case a switch to COMIS is required — dual-system management increases error risk.

P2
Group Allotment Coordination Complexity

Configuration coordination is complex for allotments covering multiple stations or customer groups. Incorrect configuration leads to wrong capacity distribution.

P3
ACRM-COMIS Dual System Management

Some allotment configurations require operations in both systems. Synchronization delays and risk of inconsistency exist.

P4
Report Ownership Separation — Visibility Limitation

Being able to see allotment utilization performance reports in ACRM while report ownership is with the Capacity Analyst creates a dependency for operational decisions.

PAIN POINT SUMMARY
The most critical challenge is that exception scenario configurations cannot be fully handled in ACRM — this means a mandatory switch to COMIS and dual-system management. Group allotment coordination is the second major challenge. The report ownership separation creates a dependency in operational decision-making processes.

The highest priority opportunity is moving exception scenario support into ACRM — this single change reduces both error risk and dual-system management. Group conflict control should be completed as MVP. In the long term, a unified allotment platform should be targeted.
