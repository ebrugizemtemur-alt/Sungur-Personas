⚠️ EKSİK BİLGİ SORUNUMISSING INFORMATION PROBLEM

"Sometimes shipper is put as just a dot. There is no information about shipper/consignee. Even though it is told to the user, they do it by saying 'I will enter later'." Sonuç: Tarama yapılamıyor, yaptırım riski artıyor. "Sometimes shipper is put as just a dot. There is no information about shipper/consignee. Even though it is told to the user, they do it by saying 'I will enter later'." Result: Cannot screen, sanction risk increases.

ÖzetSummary

Booking oluştuğunda Accuity'e gönderilir → OPEN status → Yaptırım listelerinde tarama → Match varsa REJECTED, yoksa APPROVED. Kritik sorun: Shipper/consignee "." olarak girilirse tarama yapılamaz. Ayrıca uçuş bilgisi Accuity'de yok, önceliklendirme zorlaşıyor. When booking is created, sent to Accuity → OPEN status → Screening against sanction lists → REJECTED if match, APPROVED if no match. Critical issue: If shipper/consignee entered as ".", cannot screen. Also no flight info in Accuity, prioritization becomes difficult.
