Persona ProfilePersona Profili

Who initiates pricing requests?Fiyatlandırma taleplerini kim başlatır?

Profile SummaryProfil Özeti

Sales Managers are the primary trigger for pricing processes. They receive customer/agent requests for special pricing, submit spot and contract requests through ACRM, apply discounts within their KTSY-defined authority, and negotiate with customers. They work under deadline pressure (e.g., promos must be requested before the 20th of each month) and need quick turnaround to win competitive deals. Satış Yöneticileri fiyatlandırma süreçlerinin birincil tetikleyicisidir. Özel fiyatlandırma için müşteri/acente taleplerini alır, ACRM üzerinden spot ve kontrat talepleri gönderir, KTSY tanımlı yetkileri dahilinde indirim uygular.
