The RM Analyst sits at the strategic heart of pricing decisions. They define MCR (Minimum Contributive Revenue) and Hurdle rates that act as absolute price floors. They configure auto-approval and auto-rejection rules in ACRM based on capacity, occupancy, forecast, and no-show data. Unlike the Pricing Specialist who manages static tariffs, the RM Analyst controls dynamic pricing behavior. They have authority to approve prices below MCR in special cases, a power not available to Sales or Pricing teams. RM Analisti fiyatlandırma kararlarının stratejik merkezinde yer alır. Mutlak fiyat tabanı görevi gören MCR (Minimum Katkı Geliri) ve Hurdle oranlarını tanımlar.

The RM Analyst configures auto-approval rules in ACRM that evaluate spot requests against MCR, capacity, and business rules. Auto-approval percentage is configurable and varies by route/product - not a fixed 70-80%. If requested price ≥ MCR + auto-approve threshold, it's auto-approved. If below MCR, it's auto-rejected OR sent to RM for manual review. MCR calculation is: Bid Price + Hurdle = MCR. RM has unique authority to approve prices below MCR (override). Special cases like Allotment (no MCR check, only access rate) and Super Contract (fixed price, no RM logic) bypass normal flow. RM Analisti, spot taleplerini MCR, kapasite ve iş kurallarına göre değerlendiren otomatik onay kurallarını ACRM'de yapılandırır. Otomatik onay yüzdesi yapılandırılabilir ve rota/ürüne göre değişir - %70-80 sabit değildir. MCR altında onaylamak için RM'nin özel yetkisi vardır.

The most critical pain point is duplicated pricing logic between COMIS and ACRM, requiring manual configuration in both systems. Rule conflict risk increases with configurability - "business users may define overlapping rules." Excel-based rule uploads add manual overhead for strategic controls. Price breakdown visibility is limited, making profitability analysis difficult. Booking update scenarios create complex repricing calculations with weighted averages. En kritik sıkıntı COMIS ve ACRM arasında çoğaltılmış fiyatlandırma mantığıdır, her iki sistemde manuel yapılandırma gerektirir.
