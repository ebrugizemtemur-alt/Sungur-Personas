TRM · RFS SCHEDULE PLANNER · PERSONA #1

RFS Schedule Planner

Road Feeder Service planning specialist who manages truck schedules extending the air network into inland areas. Creates, maintains, and optimizes truck routes with intermodal connections to flights. Responsible for Excel imports and lane management.

"Truck routes and timing are maintained including departure/arrival schedules and transit times. RFS capacity is coordinated with connecting flights to ensure intermodal transfers."
Unit: RFS/Truck Planning Dept.

Pain Points & Risk

Main challenges, severity, root causes

P1
Duplicate Lane Creation from Excel Reprocessing

Same Excel file reprocessed creates duplicate lanes without idempotent merge logic.

P2
No Specific Vendor Definition

Current system divides trucks as TRUCK (unknown capacity) and TRKSCH (known capacity) without proper vendor management.

P3
Default Capacity Dependency

Real truck capacity is often unavailable; planning continues with default capacity values.

P4
Excel Validation Gaps

Time logic errors, invalid lanes, overlapping effective dates require manual review before publication.

P5
Future Multi-Modal Support

System needs to support railway and seaway in addition to truck - requires unified transport model.

🔍 Discovery Needed

Pain points derived from BRD requirements gaps. User interviews needed to validate severity and uncover additional pain points from daily operations.

Summary

Critical pain points relate to data quality (duplicate lanes, vendor definition gaps) and capacity visibility limitations. Future-proofing for multi-modal transport is a strategic concern.

BRD: "There is no specific vendor definition in the current system but truck schedules divided as TRUCK (unknown capacity information) and TRKSCH (known capacity information)."
