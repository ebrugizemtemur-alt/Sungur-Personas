Pain Points & Risk

Reference: Pain Point Analysis · Risk Analysis · Exception Handling
P1
Segment Capacity Confusion

Multi-leg flight capacity display shows same total for all segments, potentially misleading sales users. Same total aircraft capacity shown for all legs of multi-leg flights.

P2
Missing Truck Flights

If there is a truck flight that should exist but does not appear in the system, sales users must escalate to station staff for resolution.

P3
Allotment Understanding

Understanding allotment concepts (station vs customer, advantages) requires domain knowledge that may not be fully developed in sales teams.

🔍 ROOT CAUSE

Multi-leg flight capacity display shows same total for all segments, potentially misleading sales users. Understanding allotment concepts requires domain knowledge that may not be fully developed in sales teams.

Note: Risk impact is limited since Sales Users have read-only access

Sales User pain points are primarily informational: segment capacity confusion on multi-leg flights, missing truck flights requiring escalation, and understanding complex allotment concepts. Impact is lower than operational users since they don't execute actions.
