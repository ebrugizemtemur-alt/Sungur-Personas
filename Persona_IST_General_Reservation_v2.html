Root Cause Analysis

Manual email-based workflows (CW, CGOAPR) create bottlenecks and audit gaps. Multi-leg visibility loss stems from monitor screen architecture not supporting segment-level status. No AWB-based embargo granularity forces system-wide toggle affecting all concurrent reservations.

Summary

6 pain points identified. Most critical: multi-leg tracking loss (P1) and CW manual process (P2). Manual processes (email approval, CW calculation) increase operational burden and create compliance risks.

P1 Multi-leg Reject: "When segment rejected, capacity teams lose track on monitor flight screen. They cancel instead of reject."
P2 CW Manual: "CW value doesn't auto-reflect in COMIS. Email correspondence determines CW kg. Manual entry to Adjusted Weight."
P3 Capacity Approval: "5-10 ton threshold, manual consultation required before confirm."
P4 Time Control: "No system control for 30-45min (narrow) / 75-120min (wide) pre-departure cutoff."
P5 HS Code: "If not entered for Accuity destinations, reservation falls to Queue status."
P6 28-Day Limit: "No pre-booking for IST departures beyond 28 days."
MoM-2025-12-12: "Pain point: In multi-leg flights; whichever segment is rejected should be rejectable but when rejected; capacity teams lose track of it on the monitor flight screen."

AI Suitability Rationale:
• CW calculation based on email archive — AI can learn patterns but approval must be human
• Accuity override = compliance risk, definitely human decision
• Multi-leg segment cancel affects other stations, shouldn't be in AI authority
