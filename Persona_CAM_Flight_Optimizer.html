🔴 Multi-screen Navigation

Need to navigate between multiple screens (CAP145, FLT002, etc.) to get complete picture. No consolidated view of all relevant information.

P3
Segment Capacity Visibility

Difficulty seeing segment-level capacity for multi-leg flights. Same total aircraft capacity shown for all legs.

Netline-COMIS Mismatch

Discrepancies between Netline and COMIS data require cross-system validation and manual reconciliation.
